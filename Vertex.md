In the context of [[Graph|graphs]], a vertex represents an objects within a given graph. 

The collection of all vertices in a graph $G$ is given by $|V|$

## Degree

The number of edges connected to a vertex is called the vertex's [[Degree (Graphs)|degree]]

## Strength

For an [[Graph#Undirected graphs|undirected]] [[Graph#Weighted graphs|weighted graph]], we can find the strength of a vertex $s_{i}$ by:

$$
s_{i}=\sum_{i}w_{ij}
$$
