In the context of [[Graph|graphs]], an edge represents some relationship between two [[Vertex|vertices]].

The collection of all vertices in a graph $G$ is given by $E$, with $|E|$ being the number of edges in the graph.

## Density

A graph has a maximum number of edges of $\displaystyle\frac{|V|(|V|-1)}{2}$, and so we can determine the [[Density (Graphs)|density]] of that graph.
