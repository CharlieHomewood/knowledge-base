A small-world network is a [[Graph|graph]] with a high [[Clustering Coefficient|clustering coefficient]] and a low average distance between [[Vertex|vertices]]. 

## Formula

We can measure the small-world index of a graph as follows:

$$
\sigma=\frac{C/C_{\text{rand}}}{L/L_{\text{rand}}}
$$

Where:

 - $C/C_{\text{rand}}$ is the normalised clustering coefficient of the graph
 - $L/L_{\text{rand}}$ is the normalised [[Path Length (Graphs)|path length]] of the graph
