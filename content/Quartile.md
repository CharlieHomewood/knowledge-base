A quartile is a proportion of a set of data which contains $25\%$ of the values. Thus, a set of data can be divided into $4$ equally-sized parts. 

We typically refers to the $1^{st}$, $2^{nd}$ and $3^{rd}$ quartiles of a dataset, whereby:

 - The $1^{st}$ quartile is upper-bounded by the $25^{th}$ [[Percentile|percentile]] and contains the lowest $25\%$ of the data
 - The $2^{nd}$ quartile corresponds to the [[Median|median]] and contains the lowest $50\%$ of the data 
 - The $3^{rd}$ quartile is the $75^{th}$ percentile and contains the lowest $75\%$ of the data
