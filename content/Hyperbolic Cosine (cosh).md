The hyperbolic cosine is a type of [[Hyperbolic Function|hyperbolic function]] defined as the [[Arithmetic Mean|mean average]] of the sum of the exponential of $x$ and its inverse.

## Formula

$$
\cosh x=\frac{e^{x}+e^{-x}}{2}=\frac{e^{2x}+1}{2e^{x}}
$$
