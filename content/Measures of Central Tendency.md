A measure of central tendency is a summary statistic for a set of data which provides information about the central or most common value in that dataset.

Examples of measures of central tendency include:

 - [[Arithmetic Mean]]
 - [[Geometric Mean]]
 - [[Harmonic Mean]]
 - [[Median]]
 - [[Mode]]
