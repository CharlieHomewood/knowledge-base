The robustness of a [[Graph|graph]] refers to the level of redundancy in the graph: the ability of the graph to retain its structure when [[Vertex|vertices]] or [[Edge|edges]] are removed.

We can measure the robustness of a graph by observing how the proportion of the size of the [[Component (Graphs)#Giant Component|giant component]] decreases as vertices or edges are removed.
