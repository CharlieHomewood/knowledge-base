The expectation value $E(X)$ is the sum of the possible values $x_{i}$ a [[Random Variable|random variable]] $X$ can take, weighted by the individual probabilities $p_{i}$ of each of those outcomes.

### Formulae

Let $X$ be a random variable, $E(X)$ be its expectation value and $f(x)$ be some [[Probability Mass Function|probability mass function]] or [[Probability Density Function|probability density function]].

For [[Probability Distribution#Discrete Distributions|discrete probabilities distributions]]:

$\displaystyle E(X)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}~f(x_{i})$

For [[Probability Distribution#Continuous Distributions|continuous probability distributions]]:

$\displaystyle E(X)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}x~f(x)~dx$
