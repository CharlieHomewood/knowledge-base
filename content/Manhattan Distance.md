Manhattan distance is the sum of the absolute differences of each co-ordinate in some $n$-dimensional space.

## Formula

For some $D$-dimensional space, we can find the Manhattan distance between $x_{i}$ and $x_{n}$ by:

$$
\text{dist}(x_{i}, x_{n})=\sum_{d=1}^{D}|x_{\displaystyle i_{d}} - x_{\displaystyle n_{d}}|
$$
