A triplet is structure in a [[Graph|graph]], where three [[Vertex|vertices]] are [[Connectedness (Graphs)|connected]] by [[Edge|edges]].

## Types

### Open Triplet 

An open triplet consists of three vertices which are connected by at least two edges.

### Closed Triplet

A closed triplet is where all three vertices respectively share an edge.

 - $1$ closed triplet is equivalent to $3$ triangles. For any set of 3 vertices $\{A,B,C\}$, we can express three versions of the same triangle, each cantered at a vertex in the set: $(ABC, BCA, CBA)$.
