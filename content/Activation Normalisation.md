Activation normalisation is used in [[Neural Network|neural networks]] to standardise the pre-activation outputs of neurons in the network. This technique helps to reduce exploding and vanishing gradients, improving network performance and speed of convergence.

Different approaches can be taken to apply activation normalisation:
 - [[Batch Normalisation (BatchNorm)]]
 - [[Layer Normalisation (LayerNorm)]]
