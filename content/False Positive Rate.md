The false positive rate is a metric used to evaluate the performance of a [[Classification Algorithms|classification]] [[Machine Learning|machine learning]] [[Algorithms|algorithm]], define as the proportion of [[False Positives|false positives]] to the total number of actual negatives.

## Notation

The recall of a model is given by the ratio between the number of false positives and the total number of actual negatives (i.e. false positives + [[True Negatives|true negatives]]):

$$
\text{FPR}=\frac{FP}{FP+TN}
$$
