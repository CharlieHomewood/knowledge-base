A residual is defined as the difference between some observed value and the estimated value.

Rather than just finding the residual of each pair of observed-estimated values, we often summarise the deviation between estimates values and observed values of the entire dataset by using a summary statistic like the [[Sum of Squared Residuals|sum of squared residuals]] or [[Mean Squared Error|mean squared error]].

## Formula

A residual $r_{i}$ is the difference between an observed value $y_{i}$ and the estimated value $\hat{y_{i}}$,:

$$
r_{i}=y_{i}-\hat{y_{i}}
$$
