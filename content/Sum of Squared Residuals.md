The sum of squared [[Residual|residuals]] is a measure of the overall amount of deviation between the observed values in a dataset and the estimated values made by a model, which is simply the sum of each squared observed-estimated value.

Squaring these residuals ensures that positive and negative signs do not cancel out. Since we only care about the magnitude of the deviation, not the direction per-se, we square each residual to ensure it is a positive value.
