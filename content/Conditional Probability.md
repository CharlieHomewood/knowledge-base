Conditional probability measures the probability of some event $B$, given that some other event $A$ has occurred.

## Formula

For events $A$ and $B$, the probability of $A$ given that $B$ has occurred is found by:

$$
P(A|B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}
$$
