Covariance measures how two [[Random Variables|random variables]] vary together when they share a [[Joint Distribution|joint distribution]].

## Formula

For two random variables $X$ and $Y$, their covariance is given by:

$$
\text{cov}(X,Y)=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i}(x_{i}-\bar{x})(y_{i}-\bar{y})
$$
