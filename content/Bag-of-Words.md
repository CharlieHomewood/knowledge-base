In [[Natural Language Processing|natural language processing]], a bag-of-words model is a method for representing word frequencies in documents.

## Notation

For some number of documents $D$, and some number of unique words $W$, we can express the word frequencies using a $D \times W$ [[Matrix|matrix]]:

$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
 & W_1 & \dots & W_n \\ \hline
D_1 & 136 & \dots & 31 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
D_n & 203 & \dots & 472
\end{array}
$$
