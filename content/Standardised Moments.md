Standardised moments of the [[Probability Distribution|probability distribution]] of a [[Random Variable|random variable]] are the [[Central Moments|central moments]] of that distribution, standardised by some integer power of the [[Standard Deviation|standard deviation]] of that distribution.

The $1^{st}$ and $2^{nd}$ standardised moments are equal to 0 and 1, respectively.

The $3^{rd}$ and $4^{th}$ standardised moments are equivalent to the [[Skewness|skewness]] and [[Kurtosis|kurtosis]], respectively.

## Formula

The $k^{th}$ standardised moment $\bar{\mu}_{k}$ of the probability distribution of a random variable is given by:

$$
\bar{\mu}_{k} = \frac{E[(X-\mu)^{k}]}{E[(X-\mu)^{2}]^{k/2}}
$$

Where $E[(X-\mu)^{2}]^{k/2}$ is the $k^{th}$ power of the standard deviation of that distribution $\sigma^{k}$.
