A langrage multiplier is used in optimisation tasks to maximise or minimise a function, given some set of constraints.

## Formula

The Lagrangian $\mathcal{L}(x,\lambda)$ can be expressed as:

$$
\mathcal{L}(x,\lambda)=f(x)+\langle\lambda,g(x)\rangle
$$

Where $\langle\lambda,g(x)\rangle$ is the [[Inner Product|inner product]] of $\lambda$ and $g(x)$.

We then take the partial [[Derivative (Calculus)|derivatives]] of the Lagrangian, with respect to each variable, set them all equal to $0$ and solve for each variable.
