For a [[Matrix|matrix]] equation $Ax=b$, the normal equation minimises the [[Sum of Squared Differences|sum of square differences]] between each side of the equation.

## Formula

Given some matrix equation $Ax=b$, the normal equation is given by:

$$
A^{\top}Ax=A^{\top}b
$$
