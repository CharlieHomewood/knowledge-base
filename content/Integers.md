Integers refers to any whole number - $0$, all positive whole numbers (i.e. [[Natural Numbers|natural numbers]]) $\{1,2,3,\dots\}$ and all negative whole numbers $\{-1,-2,-3,\dots\}$.

## Notation

The [[Set|set]] of integers is denoted $\mathbb{Z}$.
