[[Degree (Graphs)|Degree]] heterogeneity refers to the amount of [[Variance|variance]] in the degree of [[Vertex|vertices]] in a [[Graph|graph]].

## Formulae

The variance of the [[Degree Distribution|degree distribution]] of a graph can be expressed as:

$$
\text{Var}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left(k_{i} - \langle k\rangle \right)^{2}
$$

We can also consider the degree heterogeneity parameter $\kappa$:

$$
\kappa =\frac{\langle k^{2} \rangle}{\langle k \rangle^{2}}
$$
