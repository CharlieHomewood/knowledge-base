The norm of a [[Vector|vector]] is the distance from the origin to that vector

## Formulae

Formulae for the norm of a vector depends on the definition of "distance" in use.

For a [[Euclidean Distance|Euclidean distance]], we find the square root of the sum of the squared elements of the vector:

$$
||x||_{2}=\sqrt{x_{1}^{2}+\dots+x_{n}^{2}}
$$

For [[Manhattan Distance|Manhattan distance]], we simply sum the absolute values of each element of the vector:

$$
||x||_{1}=|x_{1}|+\dots+|x_{n}|
$$
