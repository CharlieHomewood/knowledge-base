A complex number is a real number $a$ which is added to some [[Imaginary Number|imaginary number]] $bi$.

## Notation

The set of complex numbers is denoted $\mathbb{C}$.

We can express any complex number using the form:

$$
a+bi
$$

Where $i$ is the [[Imaginary Unit|imaginary unit]], and $bi$ is an imaginary number.
