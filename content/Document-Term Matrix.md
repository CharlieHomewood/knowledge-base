A document-term [[Matrix|matrix]] is used to outline the frequency of term (words, where [[Polysemy|polysemous]] words appear more than once to reflect each distinct meaning) occurrences across some number of documents.

Each row $m$ is a unique term in the overall corpus of documents, whilst each column $n$ is a unique document.
