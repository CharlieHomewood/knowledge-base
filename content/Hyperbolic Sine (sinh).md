The hyperbolic sine is a type of [[Hyperbolic Function|hyperbolic function]] defined as the [[Arithmetic Mean|mean average]] of the difference of the exponential of $x$ and its inverse.

## Formula

$$
\sinh x=\frac{e^{x}-e^{-x}}{2}=\frac{e^{2x}-1}{2e^{x}}
$$
