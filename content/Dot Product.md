Given two [[Vector|vectors]] $u$ and $v$, the dot product is the sum of the pairwise products of each element of $u$ and $v$.

## Properties

The two vectors $u$ and $v$ must have the same number of elements in order for their dot product to be found.

## Notation 

The dot product of two vectors $u$ and $v$, each consisting of $n$ elements, is given by:

$$
u \cdot v=\sum_{i=1}^{n}u_{i}v_{i}=u_{1}v_{1}+\dots+u_{n}v_{n}
$$

We can also express the dot product as the product of the length (i.e. the [[Norm|norm]]) of each vector $||u||\times||v||$ multiplied by the [[Cosine|cosine]] of the angle between them $\cos{\theta}$:

$$
u \cdot v = ||u||~||v|| \cos{\theta}
$$
