## [[Derivative (Calculus)|Differentiation]]

If we have the sum or difference of two functions $af(x)$ and $bg(x)$, the derivative, with respect to $x$, of this sum/difference is equal to the sum/difference of each function's respective derivative:

$$
\frac{d}{dx}[af(x) \pm bg(x)]=af^{\prime}(x) \pm bg^{\prime}(x)
$$

## [[Integral|Integration]]

If we have the sum or difference of two functions $f(x)$ and $g(x)$, the integral, with respect to $x$ of this sum/difference is equal to the sum/difference of each function's respective integrals:

$$
\int[af(x) \pm bg(x)]~dx=\int af(x)~dx \pm \int bg(x)~dx
$$
