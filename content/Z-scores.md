A $z$-score is a normalised value used to indicate the number of [[Standard Deviation|standard deviations]] away from the [[Arithmetic Mean|mean]] a given raw value is.

## Formula

For some mean $\mu$ and some standard deviation $\sigma$, the $z$-score of some data point $x$ is given by:

$$
z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}
$$
