Given two [[Vector|vectors]] $u$ and $v$ from any [[Vector Space|vector space]], the inner product refers to a set of functions which multiply $u$ and $v$ to yield a [[Scalar|scalar]]. 

## Properties

An inner product must satisfy the following properties:

1. Linearity in the first argument: $\langle au,v\rangle=a\langle u,v \rangle$
2. Symmetry: $\langle u,v \rangle = \langle v,u\rangle$
3. Positive-definiteness: $\langle v,v\rangle \ge 0 ~\land~ \langle v,v\rangle=0 \Longleftrightarrow v=0$
