Antonymy describes a relationship between words which have opposite meanings.

Substituting antonyms would lead to a contradiction

 - E.g. The food is *hot* $\to$ The food is *cold*

However, in some sense antonyms are very similar in meaning. Antonyms often refers to opposite degrees of the same phenomenon, rather than entirely unrelated concepts (e.g. *hot* and *cold* both refer to different degrees of temperature, rather than being entirely unrelated).
