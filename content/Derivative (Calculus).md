A derivative of a function $f(x)$ is a new function $\displaystyle\frac{dy}{dx}$ which represents the change in the output of a function, due to a change to one of the inputs of that function. 

## Formula

The general formula for the derivative of $f(x)$, denoted $f^{\prime}(x)$, can be expressed as the [[Limit|limit]] as $h$ tends to $0$:

$$
f^{\prime}(x)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}
$$

Not all functions can be differentiated at every point. Such non-differentiable points would have no finite solution for the above limit.
