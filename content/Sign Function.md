A sign function is a function which maps [[Real Numbers|real number|real-valued]] inputs to either $-1$ or $1$ depending on whether the input value is negative or positive.

## Formula

For some real number $x$, the sign function will output $-1$ or $1$ depending on whether $x$ is negative (or $0$) or positive:

$$
\text{sign}(x)=\begin{cases}1&x>0 \\ -1&x\le0\end{cases}
$$
