Oversampling is a technique used to address [[Class Imbalance|class imbalance]] in the dataset used to train a [[Classification Algorithms|classification]] [[Machine Learning|machine learning]] [[Algorithms|algorithm]] by increasing the number of samples in the minority classes.

Oversampling can be achieved by duplicating random samples from the minority classes such that the number of samples equals that of the majority class.
