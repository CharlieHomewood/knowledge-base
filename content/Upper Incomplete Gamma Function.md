The upper incomplete [[Gamma Function|gamma function]] is a type of gamma function with a lower [[Integral|integral]] limit of $x$, rather than $0$.

## Formula

$$
\Gamma(s,x)=\int_{x}^{\infty}t^{s-1}e^{-t}\,dt
$$
