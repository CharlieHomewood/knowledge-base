## Formula

For events $A$ and $B$, the probability of $B$ given that $A$ has occurred is:

$$
P(B|A)=\frac{P(A|B)P(B)}{P(A)}
$$
Where:

 - $P(B|A)$ is the [[Conditional Probability|conditional probability]] of $B$, given $A$. This is also known as the [[Posterior Probability|posterior probability]].
 - $P(B)$ is the [[Prior Probability|prior probability]] of $B$.
 - $P(A|B)$ is the conditional probability of $A$, given $B$, also known as the [[Likelihood Function|likelihood]] of $A$.
 - $P(A)$ is the probability of $A$.
