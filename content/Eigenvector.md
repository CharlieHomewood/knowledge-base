An eigenvector of a [[Square Matrix|square matrix]] $A$ is a [[Vector|vector]] $v$ such that [[Matrix Multiplication|multiplying]] the $v$ with $A$ is equivalent to simply multiplying $v$ by some [[Scalar|scalar]] $\lambda$ (the [[Eigenvalue|eigenvalue]] of $v$). 

Thus, an eigenvector of $A$ is merely scaled by $A$ when multiplied by it, rather than having its direction changed.

## Notation

A vector $v$ is an eigenvector of matrix $A$ if:

$$
Av=\lambda v
$$

Where:

 - $\lambda$ is the eigenvalue of $v$
