A box plot is a data visualisation used to summarise the [[Measures of Central Tendency|central values]] and [[Measures of Dispersion|dispersion]] of a set of data.

## Features

![[image-2.png|399x148]]

 - The central orange line corresponds to the [[Median|median]] of the data.
 - The left and right edges of the central box are the $1^{st}$ and $3^{rd}$ [[Quartile|quartiles]]. Thus, the width of this box is the [[Interquartile Range|interquartile range]].
 - The outer vertical lines are the minimum and maximum values in the data, which together represent the [[Range|range]] of the data.
