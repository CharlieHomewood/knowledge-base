An integral of a function is a generalisation of a sum to continuous values, which can be used to calculate the area or volume under a function between two points.

## Formula

### As the limit of Riemann sums

The general formula for the integral of $f(x)$ can be expressed as the limit of a summation of the areas of vertical slices below the function, whose width tends to $0$ and the number of slices tends to $\infty$. This is also known as the limit of Riemann sums:

$$
\int_{a}^{b} f(x)~dx=\lim_{n \to \infty}\sum_{i=1}^{n}f(x_{i}^{*})~\Delta x_{i}
$$
