The interquartile range (IQR) of a set of data refers to the middle $50\%$ of that data. Specifically, the IQR is the difference between the $25^{th}$ and $75^{th}$ percentile of the data.

The IQR is often visualised via a [[Box Plot|box plot]], where the IQR corresponds to the "box".
