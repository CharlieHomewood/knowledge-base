Transitivity is the probability that two [[Vertex|vertices]], which are adjacent to some reference vertex, in a [[Graph|graph]] share an [[Edge|edge]] between them.

## Formula

The transitivity $T$ can be found by dividing the number of [[Triplet (Graphs)#Closed Triplet|closed triplets]] by the total number of [[Triplet (Graphs)|triplets]]:

$$
T=\frac{N_{\text{closed triplets}}}{N_{\text{all triplets}}}
$$
