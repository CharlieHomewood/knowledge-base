Synonymy describes a relationship between words where each word means the same thing as every other word.

Words are synonymous if they can be substituted in all possible context without changing the meaning of the sentence.

True synonyms are very rare. Instead, we colloquially refer to words as synonyms if they have one or more shared [[Word Sense|word senses]].
