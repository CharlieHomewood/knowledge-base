Given some nested functions $f(g(x))$, the chain rule is a method for [[Derivative (Calculus)|differentiating]] such a function.

## Formula

If we have some function $y=f(u)$ and $u=g(x)$, the derivative of $y$ with respect to $x$ can be found by:

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy}{du}\frac{du}{dx}
$$
