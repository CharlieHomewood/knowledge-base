The intersection of $n$ [[Set|sets]] $\{A_{1},\dots,A_{n}\}$ is the set of all elements which are present in every set under consideration.

## Notation

For two sets $A$ and $B$, the intersection is expressed as:

$$
A \cap B=\{x:x \in A \text{ and } x \in B\}
$$

For $n$ sets $\{A_{1}, \dots, A_{n}\}$, the intersection is given by:

$$
\bigcap_{i=1}^{n}A_{i}=A_{1}\cap A_{2}\cap\dots\cap A_{n-1}\cap A_{n}=\{x~|~x \in A_{i}~~~\forall_{i} \in \{1,\dots,n\}\}
$$
