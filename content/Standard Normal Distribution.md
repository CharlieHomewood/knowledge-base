The standard normal distribution is a [[Normal Distribution|normal distribution]] with [[Arithmetic Mean|mean]] $\mu=0$ and [[Variance|variance]] $\sigma^{2}=1$

This standard normal distribution is **incredibly useful**, allowing us to standardise any normal distribution by converting its values to [[Z-scores|z-scores]], allowing us to find the probabilities associated with certain ranges of values in our distribution.

Without this standard normal distribution, such probabilities would need to be uniquely found for each unique normal distribution - of which there are infinitely many.

## Notation

The standard normal distribution is denoted by:

$$
\mathcal{N}(0,1)
$$
