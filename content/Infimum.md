The infimum of a [[Partially Ordered Set|partially ordered]] [[Set|set]] is the greatest value such that it is less than or equal to all the elements of a set.

## Notation

For some set $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, the value $m$ is the infimum of $S$ if it is the greatest value which less than or equal to all elements of $S$:

$$
\inf S=m
$$
