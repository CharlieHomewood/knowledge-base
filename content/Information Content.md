Information content is a measure used to convey how "surprising" a particular event is.

## Formula

Given some probability $\mathbb{P}(x)$, and some real number $b \in \mathbb{R}, b > 1$ the information content is the negative $log_{b}$ of this probability:

$$
\text{IC}(x)=-log_{b}(\mathbb{P}(x))
$$
