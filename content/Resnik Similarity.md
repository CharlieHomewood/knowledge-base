The Resnik similarity of two concepts is defined as the [[Information Content|information content]] of the two concept's [[WordNet#Lowest Common Subsumer|lowest common subsumer]].

## Formula

$$
\text{sim}_{\text{res}}(c_{1}, c_{2})=\text{IC}(\text{LCS}(c_{1}, c_{2}))
$$
