The error function is a function used to express the integral of the [[Gaussian Function|Gaussian function]]. 

This function is also involved in the formula for the [[Cumulative Distribution Function|cumulative distribution function]] of the [[Normal Distribution|normal distribution]].

## Formula

$$
\text{erf}(z)=\frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{0}^{z}e^{-t^{2}}\,dt
$$
