Euclidean distance is the straight-line distance between two points in Euclidean space.

## Formula

The Euclidean distance between $x_{i}$ and $x_{n}$, in some $D$-dimensional Euclidean space, is given by:

$$
\text{dist}(x_{i},x_{n})=\sqrt{\sum_{d=1}^{D}(x_{\displaystyle i_{d}}-x_{\displaystyle n_{d}})^{2}}
$$
