Precision is a metric used to evaluate the performance of a [[Classification Algorithms|classification]] [[Machine Learning|machine learning]] [[Algorithms|algorithm]], defined as the proportion of [[True Positives|true positives]] to all positive classifications.

## Notation 

The precision of a model is given by the ratio between the number of true positives $TP$ and the total number of positive predictions (i.e. true positives + [[False Positives|false positives]]) $TP+FP$:

$$
\text{precision}=\frac{TP}{TP+FP}
$$
