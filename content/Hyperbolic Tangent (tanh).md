The hyperbolic tangent (tanh) is a type of [[Hyperbolic Function|hyperbolic function]] which takes some [[Real Numbers|real number]] as an input and produces an output bounded between $0$ and $1$.

## Formula

For some real number $x$, the hyperbolic tangent of $x$ is just the ratio of the [[Hyperbolic Sine (sinh)|hyperbolic sine]] of $x$ to the [[Hyperbolic Cosine (cosh)|hyperbolic cosine]] of $x$:

$$
\tanh(x)=\frac{\sinh(x)}{\cosh(x)}~~~~~x\in\mathbb{R}
$$

We can also express $\tanh(x)$ as:

$$
\tanh(x)=\frac{e^{x}-e^{-x}}{e^{x}+e^{-x}}~~~~~x\in\mathbb{R}
$$

This latter expression is commonly used when $\tanh$ is used as an [[Activation Function|activation function]] in [[Neural Network|neural networks]].
