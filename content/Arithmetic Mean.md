The arithmetic mean is a [[Measures of Central Tendency|measure of central tendency]], defined as the sum of a set of values, divided by the number of values in the set. 

In statistics, this set of values usually would be a set of observed values (e.g. the heights of 100 people, in centimetres).

Furthermore, we can also distinguish between two kinds of arithmetic means in statistics: 

 - [[Population Mean|Population mean]] (denoted $\mu$)
 - [[Sample Mean|Sample mean]] (denoted $\bar{x}$)

Other averages also exist, such as the [[Harmonic Mean|harmonic mean]] and the [[Geometric Mean|geometric mean]]. 

## Formula

We can express the arithmetic mean ($\mu$ or $\bar{x}$) of some set of values $X = \{x_{1},~...,~x_{n}\}$ as follows:

$$
\bar{x} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i} = \frac{x_{1}~+~...~+~x_{n}}{n}
$$
