PPDAC is an acronym for a type of data science project management method


> [!INFO] PPDAC
> **P**roblem: The question to be solved
> 
> **P**lan: Develop a plan of how to measure variables, study design, data collection etc.
> 
> **D**ata: Managing the data: pre-processing, cleaning etc.
> 
> **A**nalysis: Inferential statistics, data visualisation, hypothesis testing 
> 
> **C**onclusions: Interpreting results, discussing implications
