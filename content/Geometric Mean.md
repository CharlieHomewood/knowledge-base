The geometric mean of a set of values is the $n^{th}$ root of their product.

Examples of use cases for the geometric mean are describing proportional growth rates and averaging normalised values.

Other averages also exist, such as the [[Arithmetic Mean|arithmetic mean]] and the [[Harmonic Mean|harmonic mean]]. 

## Formula

For some set of values $X = \{x_{1},~...,~x_{n}\}$, the geometric mean $\text{GM}$ is given by:

$$
\text{GM} = \left(\displaystyle\prod_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}\right)^{\displaystyle\frac{1}{n}} = \displaystyle\sqrt[\displaystyle n]{x_{1}~\times~...~\times~x{n}}
$$
