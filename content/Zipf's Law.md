Ziph's law refers to an empirical phenomenon which occurs in a wide range of contexts where, by sorting a set of measurements in descending order, we find that the value of the $n$-th measurement is proportional to $1/n$.

## Examples 

### Word Frequency in a Text 

If we count the number of times each word appears in a text and sort them in descending order by word count, we find that the word count of the $2^{nd}$ most common word is about $1/2$ the value of the most common word. 

The $3^{rd}$ most common word therefore occurs $1/3$ as often as the most common word; the $4^{th}$ most common word occurs $1/4$ as often as the most common word, and so on...

This phenomenon occurs for any body of text which represent natural language.
