## Introduction

This knowledge base contains explanations of important concepts in data science which I have come across in my learning journey. My aim is to share my notes on these concepts publicly in an attempt to develop my own understanding, and to hopefully help others learn just as others help me learn!

If there's a concept you're interested in learning more about, have a look to see if it's here!

Should you find this resource useful, or have suggestions for improvements, do let me know!
