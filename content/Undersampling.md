Undersampling is a technique used to address [[Class Imbalance|class imbalance]] in the dataset used to train a [[Classification Algorithms|classification]] [[Machine Learning|machine learning]] [[Algorithms|algorithm]] by reducing the number of samples in the majority classes.

Undersampling can be achieved by removing random samples from the majority classes such that the number of samples equals that of the minority class.
