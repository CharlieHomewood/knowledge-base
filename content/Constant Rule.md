## [[Derivative (Calculus)|Differentiation]]

In differentiation, if $f(x)$ is equal to a constant $c$ - that is, some expression which does not depend on the value of $x$ - then the derivative $f^{\prime}(x)$ is simply equal to $0$.

## [[Integral|Integration]]

In integration, if $f(x)$ is a constant $c$, then the integral, with respect to $x$, is given by:

$$
\int c~dx=cx+C
$$

Where $C$ is the constant of integration.
