A Markov chain is a stochastic (i.e. random) process where the probability of an event depends only on the current state, not any previous states.

## Notation

We can express the definition of a Markov chain symbolically. For a [[Set|subset]] $A$ of a [[Sample Space|sample space]] $\mathcal{S}$:

$$
\mathbb{P}(X_{i+1}\in A~|~X_{j}=x_{j},~0\le j < i)=\mathbb{P}(X_{i+1}\in A~|~X_{i}=x_{i})
$$
