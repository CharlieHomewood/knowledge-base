In the context of [[Graph|graphs]], if the graph is [[Graph#Weighted graphs|weighted]] then we can, for a given [[Vertex|vertex]], find the strength of that vertex.

## Formula

For an [[Graph#Undirected graphs|undirected]] [[Graph#Weighted graphs|weighted graph]], the strength $s_{i}$ of a vertex is given by:

$$
s_{i}=\sum_{i}w_{ij}
$$
