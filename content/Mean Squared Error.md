The mean squared error (MSE) is a measure of the [[Arithmetic Mean|average]] squared [[Residual|residuals]] or [[Statistical Error|error]] between the observed values in a dataset and the estimated values made by a model.

In [[Machine Learning|machine learning]], MSE is commonly used as a [[Loss Function|loss function]] in regression tasks.

## Formula

The mean squared error $\text{MSE}$ of a dataset with $N$ samples, each with an estimated value, is given by:

$$
\text{MSE}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}(y_{i}-\hat{y_{i}})^{2}
$$
