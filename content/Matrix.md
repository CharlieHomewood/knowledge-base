A matrix is an array of mathematical objects (numbers, functions, etc.) with some number of rows $m$ and some number of columns $n$.

## Notation

For some matrix $\mathbf{A}$, we express the elements $a$ of that matrix with their corresponding row index $i$ and column index $j$:

$$
\mathbf{A} = (a_{ij}) = \begin{bmatrix}  
a_{11} & a_{12} & \dots & a_{1n} \\
a_{21} & a_{22} & \dots & a_{2n} \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
a_{m1} & a_{m2} & \dots & a_{mn}
\end{bmatrix}
$$
