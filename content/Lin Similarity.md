The Lin similarity of two concepts is defined as $2$ times the [[Resnik Similarity|Resnik similarity]] of two concepts, divided by the sum of each concept's [[Information Content|information content]]

## Formula

$$
\text{sim}_{\text{lin}}(c_{1},c_{2})=\frac{2 \times \text{sim}_{\text{res}}(c_{1}, c_{2})}{\text{IC}(c_{1})+\text{IC}(c_{2})}
$$
