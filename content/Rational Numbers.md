A rational number is any number which can be expressed as the ratio between two [[Integers|integers]], provided the denominator is non-zero.

## Notation

The set of rational numbers is denoted $\mathbb{Q}$.

We can express any rational number in the form:

$$
\frac{p}{q}~~~~~\text{where }p,q \in \mathbb{Z};~q \neq0
$$
