The sum of squared differences is a measure of the discrepancy between two sets of values, elements by element.

If the squared differences being compared are predicted values to true values (i.e. [[Residual|residuals]]) in a [[Machine Learning|machine learning]] model (e.g. [[Linear Regression|linear regression]]), we refers to a particular instance of the sum of squared differences, namely the [[Sum of Squared Residuals|sum of squared residuals]].
