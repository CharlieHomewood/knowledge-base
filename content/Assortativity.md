Assortativity refers to the tendency in a [[Graph|graph]] for [[Vertex|vertices]] to connect to other similar vertices.

A group of vertices which have a higher probability of being [[Connectedness (Graphs)|connected]] together, over other vertices in the network, is known as a [[Module (Graphs)|module]].

## Mechanisms

### Homophily and Influence 

If similar nodes become more connected, then the mechanism in effect is homophily.

If, instead, more connected nodes become more similar, then influence is the mechanism at play.

## Types

### [[Degree (Graphs)|Degree]] Assortativity 

Degree assortativity is the tendency of vertices to connect to other vertices with similar degrees.

This can be computed by [[Correlation|correlating]] the degree of vertex $j$ and the [[Degree (Graphs)#Average degree|average degree]] of the neighbouring vertices.
