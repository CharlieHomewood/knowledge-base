A graph $G$  can have a number of edges between $0$ and $\displaystyle\frac{|V|(|V|-1)}{2}$ edges, where $|V|$ is the number of vertices.

The density $D$ of a graph is therefore the ratio of the number of edges to the maximum number of possible edges

## Formulae

For an undirected graph, the density is given by:

$$
D=\frac{2|E|}{|V|(|V|-1)}
$$

For a directed graph:

$$
D=\frac{|E|}{|V|(|V|-1)}
$$
